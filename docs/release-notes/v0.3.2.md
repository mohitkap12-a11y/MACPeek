# MacPeek 0.3.2

Three new utilities for containers, the GPU and local LLMs, deeper NetPeek, DNSPeek, USBPeek and ProcessPeek, and accounts for Remote management. Still free, open source (MIT), no telemetry, and MacPeek itself needs no account. Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 13 or later.

## Highlights
- **ContainerPeek**: Which containers and VMs are running, and what are they costing me? Docker and Podman containers (Docker Desktop, OrbStack, Colima, Rancher Desktop) with published ports, Compose project, CPU and memory, plus Lima, Colima, Tart and Parallels VMs and the hypervisor processes on the Mac.
- **GPUPeek**: How busy are my GPU and Neural Engine, and who is using them? GPU load, renderer/tiler use and GPU memory, the processes using the GPU with their share of GPU time, and on Apple silicon the CPU, GPU and Neural Engine power in watts.
- **ModelPeek**: Which local LLMs are loaded, and how much memory and GPU are they using? Ollama, LM Studio, MLX, llama.cpp and llamafile runners with their loaded models, memory, CPU and GPU share.
- **Four utilities go deeper**: proxies, VPNs, Tailscale and remembered Wi-Fi in NetPeek; `/etc/hosts` and `/etc/resolver` overrides in DNSPeek; Bluetooth battery levels in USBPeek; parent chain, origin, open files and "Safe to end?" in ProcessPeek.
- **Remote management accounts**: a MacPeek Remote console signed in to an account can pair this Mac into the account, so every device of that account can connect. Remote management is still off by default.

## New utilities

### ContainerPeek
- Every container per engine, running first, with image, state, published ports, Compose project and service, and the CPU and memory the engine reports.
- Start, Stop, Restart, Pause, Resume and Remove, offered only where they apply. The container is checked again by its full ID before anything runs. Remove never deletes volumes or images and never forces.
- The last 200 log lines of a container, on request.
- An engine that is installed but not running is shown as "not running", never as "no containers".

### GPUPeek
- Per GPU: utilization, renderer and tiler use, GPU cores and memory in use, with a "since opened" chart.
- Processes with a GPU connection and the share of GPU time each used since the last reading.
- macOS reports no Neural Engine utilization without administrator rights, and MacPeek never asks for them, so GPUPeek shows the Neural Engine's power draw instead and says so.

### ModelPeek
- Loaded models come from Ollama's and LM Studio's APIs on `127.0.0.1` only, or from the runner's model argument. Nothing leaves the Mac.
- Command lines are read only for processes that could be runners, and only the model argument is kept (the rest can contain API keys).

## Existing utilities
- **NetPeek**: system proxy settings, VPN services and their status, Tailscale state (exit node, peers, health) and remembered Wi-Fi networks, with plain-language notes on how each can change what works.
- **DNSPeek**: `/etc/hosts` entries and `/etc/resolver/*` files, and a note next to a lookup when one of them applies to the name.
- **USBPeek**: Bluetooth devices with their battery levels (left, right and case for AirPods), low batteries highlighted.
- **ProcessPeek**: the full parent chain, the launchd job or app that started a process (and whether launchd keeps it alive), its open files on request, and a "Safe to end?" assessment with reasons.

## Remote management (Settings > Remote management)
Off by default. While off, nothing listens or connects.

- **Accounts (protocol version 2)**: pairing still happens at the Mac with the one-time code and four words, the access is chosen at the Mac, and the Mac says when a pairing adds it to an account.
- Consoles paired before keep working and can offer "Move this Mac to your account", which the Mac announces and lets you undo.
- Devices removed from the account are refused as soon as one of its consoles hands the Mac the account's signed revocation list.
- Settings shows the account, its devices, **Undo move** and **Remove from account**.
- **Hardened pairing**: all connections share one attempt limit against the code on screen, a cancelled pairing is never confirmed, a pairing finishes exactly once, and switching Remote management off right after on no longer leaves a listener running.
- **New for consoles**: `dns.configuration`, `dns.probe` (only the Mac's own DNS servers, only valid host names), `network.extras`, `dns.overrides`, `bluetooth.devices`, `processes.origin`, `processes.openFiles`, `containers.snapshot`, `gpu.snapshot`, `models.snapshot`, and the `containers.action` action, gated like the other actions. Protocol: `docs/remote.md`.

## Changelog (v0.3.1 -> v0.3.2)

### Added
- ContainerPeek, GPUPeek and ModelPeek (see above)
- NetPeek proxies, VPNs, Tailscale and remembered Wi-Fi; DNSPeek overrides; USBPeek Bluetooth batteries; ProcessPeek parent chain, origin, open files and "Safe to end?"
- Remote management accounts (protocol version 2), with the `operator` workspace role
- Remote requests for DNS setup and probes, and for the new utilities' data
- Shared `LooseJSON`, `ExecutableLocator` and `ByteSizeText` in MacPeekCore

### Changed
- `docs/remote.md` documents protocol version 2
- CI and the release workflow run on macOS 15 runners
- `Package.resolved` is no longer tracked

### Fixed
- Remote pairing and connection handling hardened (shared attempt limit, no confirmation of a cancelled pairing, one finish per pairing, closed connections stay closed, no leftover listener)

## Upgrading
- MacPeek offers the update itself (Settings > Updates > Check for Updates…), or download **MacPeek.dmg** below.
- No new permissions or entitlements.
- Consoles paired with 0.3.0 or 0.3.1 keep working.
