# MacPeek 0.5.0

MacPeek now watches your Mac for you and remembers the last 24 hours.

## Highlights
- **A cleaner look** – one accent colour, less chrome, readable text everywhere, and a simpler Settings. Pick your theme under Settings ▸ General.
- **Alerts** – get notified about a runaway process, a memory hog, memory pressure, swap growth, heavy network traffic or
  overheating. On by default; each rule has its own switch and threshold in Settings ▸ Alerts. Review the last 50 under
  the new bell in the launcher.
- **Menu-bar readout** – CPU, memory, network, GPU and temperature right in the menu bar (off by default, turn on in
  Settings ▸ Menu bar).
- **24-hour history** – charts for 1, 6 and 24 hours of CPU, memory, swap, network, GPU, temperature and power, with a hover tooltip.
- **Temperatures, fans and power** in CPUPeek and GPUPeek.
- **Tidier process lists** – browser tabs and app helpers are grouped under one row per app.
- **Hidden-icon help** – if macOS hides MacPeek's icon (full menu bar, notch), a window shows you how to find it.

## For MacPeek Remote users
- MacPeek Remote is free for 3 Macs; tunnels on the local network are Pro, through the relay Ultra.
- Your Mac now shares its alerts, 24-hour history and sensor readings with MacPeek Remote 0.3.0.
- A Mac set up from another Mac (Migration Assistant, Time Machine) now gets its own identity. You'll be asked to add it
  to your account again.

## Notes
- Sensor readings use undocumented macOS interfaces and still need checking on more hardware. Please report odd readings.
